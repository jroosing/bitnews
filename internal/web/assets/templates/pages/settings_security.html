Allowed hosts

{{icon "warning" "mt-0.5 size-5 shrink-0"}}

The addresses people type to open bitnews, such as bitnews.lan or 192.168.1.10:9494. While sign-in is skipped for anyone, bitnews refuses requests for any other name, which stops websites from tricking your browser into talking to it. {{if .BypassOn}}Sign-in is skipped for some devices right now, so keep your own address in the list or you lock yourself out.{{else}}Needed before you can turn the sign-in options on.{{end}}

Trusted proxies

{{icon "warning" "mt-0.5 size-5 shrink-0"}}

Networks of reverse proxies in front of bitnews (nginx, Traefik, Caddy). For requests from them, bitnews believes the visitor address they report instead of the proxy's own. Leave it empty if you open bitnews directly. Listing a network that can be reached by others lets them fake their address, which breaks LAN bypass and the login rate limit.
