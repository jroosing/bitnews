{{icon "folder" "size-4 text-fg-subtle"}}Tip: keep complete and your library on one filesystem

Sonarr and Radarr import a finished download by hardlinking it into your library, and bitnews does not make those links. That only works when the complete folders and the library are on the same filesystem, for example under /data (/data/usenet, /data/torrents, /data/media). In Docker, mount /data once, not one volume per folder. The incomplete folder can live anywhere, such as a fast local disk.

bitnews checks only that each path is absolute and that the folders are not nested. Folders that do not exist yet are fine: bitnews will create missing folders when the first download needs them.
